# Proposed package — not an accepted quote

## Commercial recommendation
$450 fixed build fee, proposed $225 deposit and $225 following approval before launch. Subject to inspecting the actual store, catalog complexity, and complete intake. No agreement or payment has been verified. Separate platform subscription, domain renewal, payment processing, postage, and any approved third-party costs from the build fee.

Target 10–12 work hours; approximately $37.50–$45/hour before business costs and taxes. If scope exceeds this, reduce deliverables or revise the quote before accepting payment.

## Proposed deliverables
- Customize one free Shopify theme for a boutique brand and mobile shopping.
- Homepage, collections/navigation, About, Contact, FAQ, and client-approved shipping/returns information.
- Up to 10 products and 30 sellable variants total; client supplies product facts, photos, prices, and stock.
- Light editing of supplied descriptions, image preparation, page titles/descriptions, alt text, and social links.
- Domain connection subject to access and current DNS assessment.
- One domestic shipping setup based on verified packed weights, package dimensions, processing times, and owner decisions.
- Guide payment setup without handling private bank or identity details. Review tax settings against owner's requirements; no unsupported tax advice.
- Test mobile navigation, product variants, stock behavior, cart, shipping rates, checkout in an appropriate test mode, and notifications.
- 45-minute walkthrough and concise guide to products, orders, and shipment processing.
- One design direction, two consolidated revision rounds, and 14 days of defect corrections for agreed work after launch.

## Exclusions / scope control
Custom apps, a theme written from scratch, photography, logo creation, extensive copywriting, unrestricted product entry, international shipping complexity, ongoing marketing, unlimited revisions/support, or guaranteed sales/rankings. Extra work requires an agreed change in scope and price.

## Delivery sequence
1. Intake: inspect store and assets; confirm written scope, costs, and timeline.
2. Design: unpublished theme; review homepage and representative product page.
3. Build: finish approved catalog, content, navigation, domain, and shipping.
4. Review: two feedback rounds; owner confirms product facts and policies.
5. QA: full shopping path, mobile layouts, availability, shipping, checkout and emails.
6. Handoff: approval, final payment, authorized launch, and training.

Proposed target: 2–3 weeks after complete assets and access are received, within the requested 1–3 month window. This is not a guaranteed date.

- Exact deliverables, catalog limits, and exclusions.

- Milestones, revision/acceptance process, fee and payment schedule.

## Design hypotheses to validate
Use actual logo and photography; establish consistent typography, generous whitespace, clear collection browsing, readable product details, and a direct route to checkout. Do not invent the final palette before reviewing approved brand files. Product images need originals rather than 206px Facebook previews.
