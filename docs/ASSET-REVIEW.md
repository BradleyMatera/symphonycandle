## Suggested concept
Use a light, warm neutral page with black typography and the existing monochrome logo. Let the colorful products provide the accent colors. Lead with a real candle/product arrangement and clear shopping categories. Possible navigation: Candles, Handmade Soaps, Gifts, About, Contact — all tentative pending current catalog confirmation. Use process photographs to support the handmade story, with factual copy approved by the owner.

Do not assign scents, prices, ingredients, therapeutic claims, burn times, stock, or shipping promises based solely on photographs. Do not present older seasonal items as available without confirmation. Avoid enlarging low-resolution photos; dimensions are recorded in the manifest.
