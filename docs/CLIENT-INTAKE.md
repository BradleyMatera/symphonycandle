# Client intake checklist

A detailed intake email was saved as a Gmail draft on November 12, 2026. Sending and any later reply have not been verified in this repository task. Do not automatically send a duplicate.

## First response questions
1. Shopify URL and storefront viewing password if necessary; never ask for account credentials in email.
2. Number of products and scent/size/other variants.
3. One or two reference websites and preferred colors/styles.
4. Availability of logo, photos, descriptions, prices — no need to upload everything at this stage.
5. Domestic/international delivery, pickup options, existing shipping process.
6. Domain ownership and registrar (including whether still at GoDaddy).
7. Whether the budget includes recurring Shopify costs, plus preferred launch date.

The public domain has now been found; ask for confirmation and viewing access rather than claiming it is unknown.

## After proposal acceptance
- Use Shopify collaborator access with appropriate permissions.
- Collect original images and confirm rights/approved logo.
- Product spreadsheet: title, description, price, SKU, stock, variants, packed weight, dimensions, ingredients/materials, care and warnings.
- Confirm tax settings, fulfillment origin, shipping services/rates, processing time, pickup, returns, customer contact details, and policies.
- Owner enters payment-provider identity and banking details themselves.
- Confirm domain/DNS changes will preserve existing email services.
- Agree content delivery and review dates.
