# Project instructions

Read README.md and docs/STATUS.md before continuing. This is a public repository for a prospective client, not an approved production launch.

- Distinguish observed facts, client statements, proposals, and unknowns.
- Do not commit private correspondence, personal contact information, credentials, session cookies, tax documents, customer records, or secrets.
- Do not invent catalog facts, stock, prices, ingredients, claims, shipping promises, testimonials, or business locations.
- Preserve original downloaded asset files; put derivatives in a separate folder. Update assets/manifest.json with provenance and dimensions.
- Gallery previews are unsuitable for large production images. Do not upscale them and label them originals.
- Brand assets retain the original owner's rights. Obtain approved originals before production use.
- Prefer a free Shopify theme and no paid apps for the initial proposed build. No new purchases without explicit authorization.
- Mockups must be clearly labeled concepts; do not activate checkout, collect customer data, or publish as the official store.
- The $450 scope is an internal proposal recommendation, not accepted terms. Confirm scope after intake.
- Keep unrelated Real Mall of America products, branding, and customer data out of this project.
